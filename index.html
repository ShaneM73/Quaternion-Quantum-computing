A two-module course

From Broom Bridge to the Bloch sphere

Module I builds Hamilton’s quaternions from complex numbers up to rotations and slerp. Module II uses those same rotations to explain quantum computing, one qubit gate at a time. Take them in order, or open either one on its own.

i ↔ −iσx  ·  j ↔ −iσy  ·  k ↔ −iσz The dictionary that joins the two modules: unit quaternions are exactly the single-qubit gates in SU(2).

Hamilton’s Quaternions

Follow Hamilton’s search for a 3D number system and learn the algebra that turns space.

  • Why ij ≠ ji, and how to multiply by hand
  • Rotating vectors with q v q⁻¹ and the half angle
  • Slerp, and why it beats Euler angles
  • Unit quaternions as SU(2) and the Pauli gates

Qubits on the Sphere

Every core idea of quantum computing, drawn as rotations of the Bloch sphere with a quaternion readout.

  • Qubit states, measurement and interference
  • Gates as quaternions; three rotations suffice
  • Noise, entanglement, kickback and teleportation
  • Grover search, gate synthesis and error correction
